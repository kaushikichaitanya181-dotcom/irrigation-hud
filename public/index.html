PROBLEM STATEMENT
PROBLEM AND NEED
Plants frequently suffer stress or die due to prolonged periods of dryness, yet manual watering creates its own set of challenges, often leading to overwatering, wasted water resources, and degraded soil conditions over time.[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)
HUMAN & TIMER LIMITATIONS
Relying on human memory inevitably causes inconsistent watering schedules[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). While simple timer-based systems offer automation by operating at predetermined intervals, they function without real feedback—running regardless of whether soil is already saturated.[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)
SYSTEM DEMAND
An intelligent system is needed that actively reads the plant's immediate environment, removing human error and ensuring water is delivered based on actual soil conditions rather than fixed schedules.[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)

INNOVATIVE SOLUTION
CLOSED-LOOP PROCESS
Establishes a reliable, data-driven closed-loop process by continuously monitoring environmental parameters in real time including soil moisture, ambient temperature, humidity, and light levels.[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)
AUTONOMOUS TRIGGER
When sensor readings indicate conditions have dropped below optimal thresholds, the program autonomously triggers a water pump to supply the precise hydration needed and shuts off once restored.[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)
TRIPLE-SENSOR TRACKING
Integrates a multi-probe sensing array positioned across different root depths, eliminating localized dry spots and reading errors caused by uneven soil texture or improper probe placement.[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)
